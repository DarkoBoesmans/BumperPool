Last shots

    • Sink your five balls into the goal at the far end. Your goal has a ring in your colour.
    • Score and you shoot again. No goal and the turn passes.
    • Never hit your own ball first. Bounce off a cushion, a post or an opponent's ball. SP/8.1
    • No hard shots from the corners near the goal, outside the shooting lines. Hard means over 1.5 m/s: the red part of the power meter. SP/7.4
    • No hard shots along a long cushion from the 92 mm strip. SP/6.7
    • Don't park in the big triangle. A ball that starts your turn there must leave it that turn. SP/9
    • Wrong goal doesn't count. Your ball goes to a penalty spot. SP/7.2
    • After a foul your turn ends, moved balls go back and your ball goes to a penalty spot your opponent picks. SP/12
    • Best of three games, colours swap each game.
