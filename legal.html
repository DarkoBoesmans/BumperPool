Who makes this

Golfbiljart is a free, non-commercial hobby game made by Darko Boesmans in Belgium. There is no company behind it, nothing is sold and there are no ads.

Contact: open an issue on the project's GitHub page. Issues there are public, so leave out anything personal; ask for a private reply if you need one.

Privacy

The game collects as little as it can. In short: nothing about you is sent to the maker.

What stays on your device

Your player (the name you type, your look), your record, tokens, the items you own, the regulars you beat, the Wall of Shame and your settings are saved in your own browser. They never leave your device and the maker cannot see them.

Online play

Online play only works in the copy published on claude.ai. When you join a table there, the table name, the names you and your opponent type, the game itself (ball positions, shots, the referee's log) and your emotes are saved in that page's shared storage, run by Anthropic, so both players see the same table. Your Claude account is identified there by an account ID, not your name. Anyone who can open the page and types the same table name can see that table. Use a nickname, not your real name.

Hosting

The game is a static page. The server that delivers it (GitHub Pages, or claude.ai for the published copy) may keep short technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service, under its own privacy policy. The game itself adds no tracking.

What the game does not do

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no ads, no social media buttons.
  • No third-party scripts or embeds: the 3D engine and the fonts ship with the game itself.
  • No accounts, no e-mail addresses, no mailing list, so no e-mails to unsubscribe from.
  • No payments.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data is held about you, have it corrected or deleted, and complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit). As the maker holds no data about you, most requests are answered by the delete button below. For online tables, ask through the contact above.

Children

The game is set in a café where people smoke cigars and drink whisky. It is meant for players aged 16 and over. It asks for no age and collects no data about anyone, children included.

Cookies and storage

Golfbiljart sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for four things: your player and progress (golfbiljart.profile), your settings (golfbiljart.settings), sound volumes (golfbiljart.volume) and the jukebox style (golfbiljart.jukebox).

This storage is strictly necessary for features you ask for, such as keeping your progress, so the EU's rules on cookies and device storage do not require a consent banner, and there is none. If the game ever adds analytics or anything else that is not strictly necessary, it will ask first.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings, then Game, and press Delete my data. It removes your player, record, tokens, items and settings from this browser and starts the game fresh. Clearing this site's data in your browser does the same. Online tables are removed on request through the contact above.

Refunds

Nothing is sold: no purchases, no subscriptions, no in-game money for real money and no hidden costs. Tokens are only earned by playing. There is nothing to pay, so there is nothing to refund.

Accessibility

The menus work with a keyboard and a screen reader, have visible focus, labelled controls and checked colour contrast, and animations calm down when your system asks for reduced motion.

At the table: B picks one of your balls, A and D turn the aim (hold Shift for fine steps), W and S set the power, Space shoots and Esc lets go. The arrow keys set the spin, T and P change the view. The referee's decisions are read out by screen readers.

Known limits: the table itself is a 3D picture, so judging angles needs sight, and the camera is turned with a mouse or touch. If something does not work for you, tell us through the contact above.
