01

Who makes this

One person, for fun. No company, no ads, nothing for sale.

Tjoepbiljart is a free, non-commercial hobby game of Belgian tap billiards, made by Darko Boesmans in Belgium.

Questions or problems? Open an issue on the project's GitHub page.

Issues there are public, so leave out anything personal. Ask for a private reply if you need one.

02

Privacy

Nothing about you is sent to the maker.

What stays on your device

Your player (the name you type and your look), your record, tokens, items, the regulars you beat, the Wall of Shame, your settings and your language are saved in your own browser. They never leave your device and the maker cannot see them.

Online play

Online play is switched off for now. Before it comes back, this page will say exactly what it shares and with whom.

Hosting

The game is a static page, delivered by GitHub Pages or, for the copy published on claude.ai, by Anthropic. That host may keep short technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service, under its own privacy policy. The game itself adds no tracking.

What the game does not do

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no ads, no social media buttons.
  • No third-party scripts or embeds: the 3D engine and the fonts ship with the game.
  • No accounts and no e-mail addresses, so no mailing list.
  • No payments.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data is held about you, have it corrected or deleted, and complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit). The maker holds no data about you, so the delete button below answers most requests.

Children

The game is set in a café where people smoke cigars and drink whisky, so it is meant for players aged 16 and over. It asks for no age and collects no data about anyone.

03

Cookies and storage

No cookies. Five small notes in your browser, so the game remembers you.

tjoepbiljart.profile
your player and progress
tjoepbiljart.settings
your settings
tjoepbiljart.volume
sound volumes
tjoepbiljart.jukebox
the jukebox style
tjoepbiljart.lang
your language

They live in your browser's local storage and are strictly necessary for features you ask for, such as keeping your progress. EU rules on cookies and device storage need no consent banner for that, so there is none. If the game ever adds anything that is not strictly necessary, it will ask first.

Saves from before the game was called Tjoepbiljart move to these names once, the first time you play.

04

Deleting your data

One button, pressed twice, and everything is gone.

  1. In the game, open Settings.
  2. Go to the Game tab.
  3. Press Delete my data, then press it again.

It removes your player, record, tokens, items and settings from this browser and starts the game fresh. Clearing this site's data in your browser does the same.

06

Refunds

Nothing is sold, so there is nothing to refund.

No purchases, no subscriptions, no in-game money for real money and no hidden costs. Tokens are only earned by playing.

07

Accessibility

The menus work with a keyboard and a screen reader.

The menus have visible focus, labelled controls and checked colour contrast, and animations calm down when your system asks for reduced motion. Screen readers read out the referee's decisions.

At the table without a mouse

B
picks one of your balls
A D
turn the aim, with Shift for fine steps
W S
set the power
Space
shoots
Esc
lets go
↑ ↓ ← →
set the spin
T P
top view, back to your end

Known limits

The table itself is a 3D picture, so judging angles needs sight, and the camera turns with a mouse or touch. If something does not work for you, say so through the contact above.
